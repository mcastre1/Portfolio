Hello World,

My name is Miguel, and I've spent most of my life working toward one goal: becoming a software developer. I've always loved technology, problem-solving, and the process of building something from nothing. Whether it's front-end design or back-end logic, I enjoy seeing an idea grow into a real solution that people can use.

My journey hasn't been a straight line. After coming to the United States as a child, immigration and work authorization challenges made it difficult to enter the software industry, even after earning my Master's degree in Computer Science. Instead of giving up, I continued learning, building projects, and supporting myself through construction and contracting work. Those experiences taught me discipline, accountability, and the value of hard work.

Today, I finally have the opportunity to pursue the career I've been working toward for years. While I may not have a long list of corporate software positions on my résumé, I bring determination, curiosity, and a genuine passion for technology. I'm someone who enjoys learning, solving difficult problems, and creating solutions that make a difference. Most of all, I'm excited to continue growing as a developer and contribute wherever I can make a positive impact.
