About Me

When I'm not building software, you'll usually find me learning something new. I've always enjoyed understanding how things work, whether that's through coding, gaming, hands-on projects, or exploring new hobbies.

One of my biggest interests is technology and programming. Even outside of work, I spend a lot of time experimenting with new tools, frameworks, and personal projects simply because I enjoy learning. For me, software development is more than a career; it's a hobby that constantly challenges me to improve and think creatively.

I'm also a longtime gamer and anime fan. My favorite anime is Naruto, a series that inspired me with its themes of growth, perseverance, and self-improvement. When it comes to games, I've always been drawn to RPGs because of their progression systems, deep stories, and problem-solving elements. One of my favorite recent games has been Black Myth: Wukong. Lately, I've also been enjoying Tibia, a classic MMORPG known for its open-world exploration, character progression, player-driven economy, and challenging gameplay that rewards strategy and teamwork.

Trading card games have been another passion throughout my life. I started with Yu-Gi-Oh!, later spent years playing Pokémon TCG, and currently enjoy Magic: The Gathering. I've also tried newer games like Disney Lorcana, but Magic remains my favorite because of its depth and strategic complexity.

Staying active is important to me as well. Most of my exercise comes from weightlifting and running, but I've also developed a love for outdoor activities. I started mountain biking last year and look forward to spending more time on the trails once I'm fully recovered from a knee injury. Living in Utah since 2009 has also given me the chance to explore snowboarding. After my first trip in 2023, my brother and I enjoyed it so much that we've made it a tradition to hit the slopes at least once a year.

During the summer, my wife and I enjoy riding ATVs through Farmington Canyon. We usually head out early in the morning before the heat sets in, spending a couple of hours exploring the trails and enjoying the cool mountain air.

Beyond technology, I'm passionate about learning practical skills and working with my hands. Over the years, I've taken on various home improvement and side projects involving electrical work, plumbing, drywall installation, carpentry, painting, and general construction. I enjoy the challenge of solving real-world problems and creating something tangible, whether it's with code or with tools.

At my core, I'm someone who enjoys learning, building, and continuously improving. Whether it's developing software, mastering a new hobby, tackling a home project, or exploring the outdoors, I'm always looking for opportunities to grow and challenge myself.
